Automated Cat Paw Cleaning Pad

A rectangular box that sits outside my cat litter box and activates when the cat steps on it, cleaning litter from its paws before it can be tracked throughout the house. Upon activation, a pump moves water through a thick, permeable, spongy pad, effectively turning it into one big wet sponge. The addition of dynamic water makes this device far more effective than dry, static litter pads. A 14-day experiment demonstrated the device’s effectiveness. During seven days without the device, 56 grams of litter accumulated outside the litter box, compared with only 23 grams over seven days with the device. This is a 59% reduction. It is worth noting that the sponge pad required thorough rinsing after the seven-day testing period.
